Kingston

Kingston (also marketed as Kingston and the Cursed Sea) is a 2025 Indian Tamil-language fantasy horror adventure film[3] directed by Kamal Prakash, in his directorial debut. It is produced by G. V. Prakash Kumar, who also enacts in the lead role, Bhavani Sre, Vipin Agnihotri and Umesh KR Bansal, under Parallel Universe Pictures and Zee Studios. The cast also includes Divyabharathi, Elango Kumaravel, Sabumon Abdusamad and Chetan.

Running time: 2h 18m

Language: Tamil
