Dragon

Dragon is a 2025 Indian Tamil-language coming-of-age comedy drama film directed by Ashwath Marimuthu, who also wrote the story with Pradeep Ranganathan. Produced by AGS Entertainment, it stars Pradeep in the titular role, Anupama Parameswaran and Kayadu Lohar, with Mysskin, Gautham Vasudev Menon, K. S. Ravikumar and George Maryan. In the film, a college dropout deceptively secures a high paying job, but is forced to accept a major challenge to avoid being exposed.

Running time: 2h 35m

Language: Tamil
